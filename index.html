        The whole playbook

          Decision support, not diagnosis. Vital signs, neck ultrasound and the clot decision come from the HemoMind engine: values are simulated inside published ISS in-flight ranges, and the decision is NASA OCHMO-MTB-007 (rev. Jan 2026, p.14) as a pure function, including its apixaban regimen. Red flags are standard emergency signs; sleep, mood and fatigue rules are demo rules, since NASA has not published validated thresholds yet. The crew member is fictional.
