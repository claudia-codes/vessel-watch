                How the evidence is used

                • Every simulated vital sign is sampled inside a range measured on ISS crews, and the source is quoted word for word.
                • Variables with no published in-flight value are not simulated; they are entered by hand.
                • The clot decision follows NASA OCHMO-MTB-007 exactly.
                • Sleep, mood and fatigue thresholds are demo values, marked as such, because NASA has not published validated ones.
                • The crew member is fictional. The playbook still needs review by a physician.

                    The whole playbook

                      Decision support, not diagnosis. Vital signs, neck ultrasound and the clot decision come from the HemoMind engine: values are simulated inside published ISS in-flight ranges, and the decision is NASA OCHMO-MTB-007 (rev. Jan 2026, p.14) as a pure function, including its apixaban regimen. Red flags are standard emergency signs; sleep, mood and fatigue rules are demo rules, since NASA has not published validated thresholds yet. The crew member is fictional.
